Voice training

Say each spell once so the game learns your voice. Each recording is added to that spell's computer-voice references and saved in this browser, so you only do this once. You can retrain any spell later with the Voice training button.

    Your recording is filtered with the FFT to keep 80 Hz to 4 kHz, and background noise is removed (top left). Its frequencies over time (bottom left) are compared with reference recordings of the spell. Each spell has four references, spoken by different computer voices, and bottom right shows the one your voice matched best. The match is measured with normalized correlation at a range of time shifts (top right), and the peak ρ sets the spell's power.
